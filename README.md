[?] Basically an intro the services i do on ponytown.

[?] What do i make ?

1. Pony town skins
2. Pony town maps

***

[?] How long will it take ?

. Skins will take atleast 2 hours or so, though making the docs itself would be a struggle so expect having the skin and docs finished after a day or two

. Maps will take atleast 1-2 days of building.

***

[?] Where do you contact me ?

. Preferably contact me via my linked DC, request first before sending details.

. I'll most probably asked for details before accepting just to make sure i'm able to accomplish it.

[!?] What are the fee's !?
